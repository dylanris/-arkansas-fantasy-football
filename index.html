Replacement Owner Draft Position Redraw

2026 RULING

Greg and Drew shall redraw between the two available replacement-franchise draft positions. Neither owner automatically receives a position based solely on which vacant team they initially entered.

PREAMBLE

The Arkansas Fantasy Football League, hereafter referred to as the AFFL, exists to provide a competitive, fair, entertaining, and lasting fantasy football league for its members.

The purpose of this Constitution & Bylaws is to establish clear expectations, preserve league history, prevent disputes whenever possible, and provide a fair process for resolving situations when the rules do not provide an obvious answer.

All owners are expected to compete in good faith, manage their teams throughout the season, respect league decisions, and protect the long-term integrity of the AFFL. Participation in the league constitutes acceptance of these rules.

ARTICLE I • League Structure

Section 1. League Size

The Arkansas Fantasy Football League shall operate as a 12-team fantasy football league unless the league approves a future change.

Section 2. League Platform

The official AFFL league shall operate through Sleeper, unless the league decides to move to another platform. Sleeper shall serve as the official record for rosters, starting lineups, matchup scores, standings, transactions, trades, draft results, playoff results, and league settings.

If a conflict exists between a screenshot, memory, verbal statement, or other unofficial source and the official Sleeper record, the Sleeper record shall control unless a platform error is confirmed.

Section 3. League Season

Each AFFL season begins when league operations officially open for the upcoming NFL season and concludes following the league championship and completion of all remaining league business.

ARTICLE II • League Membership

Section 1. Team Ownership

Each franchise shall be controlled by one owner. An owner may not control, manage, or hold ownership of more than one AFFL team at the same time.

Section 2. Good Standing

An owner is considered in good standing when that owner pays required league dues, actively manages their team, follows league rules, does not engage in collusion, responds to important league communications when necessary, and makes a reasonable effort to remain competitive.

Section 3. Returning Owners

As long as league dues are paid, owners and their teams will be welcomed back for the following season.

Returning owners shall not be removed simply to create an opening for another manager.

Section 4. New Owners

When a franchise becomes vacant, the Commissioner may search for and nominate a replacement owner. The goal should be to bring in an owner who understands football, will remain active, will pay league dues, will independently manage their team, and will strengthen the league long-term.

Section 5. Family Members and Significant Others

If the proposed new owner is a family member or significant other of an existing AFFL owner, the proposed owner must be submitted to the league for a vote. The league shall be given an opportunity to voice approval or concerns.

The Commissioner retains final authority to approve or reject the proposed owner after considering the league vote.

All owners, regardless of personal relationships, must manage their franchises independently.

ARTICLE III • League Dues

Section 1. Annual Dues

League dues shall be established before each season. The Commissioner shall announce the amount owed, accepted payment methods, payment deadline, and any additional league expenses.

Section 2. Payment Deadline

Owners are expected to pay league dues by the announced deadline. An owner who believes they may not meet the deadline should communicate with the Commissioner.

Section 3. Failure to Pay

Failure to pay league dues may result in the owner's franchise becoming vacant and available to a replacement owner. Until dues are paid, the Commissioner is not required to guarantee that owner's position in the league.

Section 4. League Funds

League funds may be used for championship payout, championship ring, league trophy, championship plaque or engraving, league website expenses, and other approved league awards or expenses. The Commissioner shall make reasonable efforts to keep league financial activity transparent.

ARTICLE IV • Keepers

Section 1. Keeper Format

The AFFL is a two-player keeper league. Each owner may keep one quarterback and one non-quarterback. An owner may not keep two quarterbacks or two non-quarterbacks.

Section 2. Keeper Eligibility

Keeper eligibility shall follow AFFL rules and the official league settings established in Sleeper.

Section 3. Keeper Deadline

The Commissioner shall announce a keeper deadline before the draft. Owners are responsible for submitting valid keepers by that deadline.

Section 4. Keeper Trades

Players eligible to be kept may be traded while league trading is open. Keeper rights transfer with the player unless otherwise prohibited by an AFFL rule.

Section 5. Replacement Owners

A replacement owner taking control of an existing franchise inherits that franchise's roster and available keeper rights. The replacement owner may select from players legally eligible to be kept on that roster.

ARTICLE V • The Draft

Section 1. Draft Format

The AFFL shall conduct an annual draft using Sleeper. Unless changed before the season, the league shall use a snake draft.

Section 2. Draft Order

Draft order shall be determined before the draft using the method established for that season. Once officially finalized, the order shall remain binding except when an owner leaves the league or another unusual circumstance requires Commissioner or Council action.

Section 3. Draft Timer

The standard AFFL draft timer shall be 30 seconds per selection.

The Commissioner may announce a different timer before a future draft if necessary.

Section 4. Missed Picks

If an owner's draft clock expires, Sleeper's automatic selection shall control. The Commissioner is not required to reverse an auto-pick caused by an owner failing to make a selection in time.

Section 5. Draft Pick Trading

Draft picks may be traded when permitted by AFFL rules and Sleeper settings. Owners are responsible for understanding the future consequences of trading draft capital.

Section 6. Vacant Teams

When an owner leaves before the draft, the Commissioner and AFFL Council shall determine the fairest way to resolve any affected draft position. The goal shall be to avoid giving an unfair advantage or disadvantage to either returning or replacement owners.

Any special decision shall be announced before the draft and recorded as an official AFFL ruling.

ARTICLE VI • Rosters and Lineups

Section 1. Starting Lineups

Owners are responsible for submitting valid starting lineups each week.

Section 2. Active Competition

Every owner is expected to make a reasonable effort to field a competitive lineup. Unacceptable behavior may include intentionally starting inactive players when reasonable alternatives exist, leaving starting positions empty, benching healthy players specifically to help another team, abandoning a roster, or deliberately losing to manipulate another team's playoff chances.

Section 3. Injuries and Mistakes

An owner shall not automatically be punished because of a surprise inactive player, late injury news, emergency, or honest lineup mistake. Intent shall be considered before disciplinary action is taken.

Section 4. Tanking

Intentional losing for the purpose of improperly helping another franchise or manipulating league results is prohibited. Legitimate roster decisions made in the owner's own competitive interest are permitted.

ARTICLE VII • Free Agency

Section 1. Official System

All player additions and drops shall be processed through Sleeper.

Section 2. First Come, First Serve

The AFFL does not use a traditional Wednesday waiver period. Once the official fantasy football week has ended and players become available under the league's Sleeper settings, player pickups are FIRST COME, FIRST SERVE.

There is no waiver-priority competition among owners once free agency opens. The first owner to legally add an available player through Sleeper receives that player.

Section 3. Timing

Sleeper's official league clock and player availability shall determine when players become eligible to be added. Owners are responsible for monitoring free agency.

Section 4. Commissioner Intervention

The Commissioner shall not reverse a legal free-agent pickup simply because another owner wanted the same player. A transaction may be corrected only when there is clear evidence of platform malfunction, Commissioner error, collusion, or another exceptional circumstance requiring correction.

ARTICLE VIII • Trades

Section 1. Owner Responsibility

Owners are free to evaluate players differently. A trade shall not be overturned simply because another owner believes one side received better value.

A bad trade is not automatically an illegal trade.

Section 2. Trade Standard

Trades shall be presumed legal unless there is credible evidence of collusion, roster dumping, outside payment, coordinated manipulation, or one owner intentionally helping another without legitimate benefit to their own franchise.

Section 3. Commissioner Review

All trade-integrity decisions shall be handled by the Commissioner. The Commissioner shall determine whether there is sufficient evidence that a trade constitutes collusion or otherwise threatens league integrity.

Section 4. No League Veto

The AFFL does not use league trade vetoes. Owners shall not vote to approve or reject another team's trade.

If the Commissioner determines that a trade is legitimate, the trade stands regardless of whether other league members believe it was fair. If the Commissioner determines that a trade constitutes collusion, the Commissioner may reverse the trade and impose additional penalties when appropriate.

Section 5. Commissioner's Own Trades

When the Commissioner is personally involved in the trade under review, the Commissioner shall not make the final decision regarding collusion.

The Co-Commissioner shall have final authority to determine whether the Commissioner's trade is legitimate.

Section 6. Trade Deadline

The official trade deadline shall follow the AFFL's established Sleeper settings unless changed before the season.

ARTICLE IX • Collusion and Competitive Integrity

Section 1. Collusion

Collusion occurs when two or more owners coordinate actions for an improper purpose that damages fair competition. Examples include intentionally giving valuable players to another team, secret agreements to return players after a game, coordinating lineups to manipulate standings, outside payments for fantasy transactions, controlling another owner's roster, or sharing control of multiple franchises.

Section 2. Penalties

Depending upon severity, penalties may include transaction reversal, loss of draft picks, loss of keeper rights, forfeiture, removal from the league, or permanent ban. Serious integrity violations may be addressed immediately by league leadership.

ARTICLE X • Inactive or Abandoned Teams

Section 1. Owner Responsibility

Owners are expected to remain reasonably active throughout the entire season regardless of record.

Section 2. Abandonment

A franchise may be considered abandoned when an owner repeatedly fails to set lineups, ignores league communication, stops making reasonable roster decisions, or states they no longer intend to participate.

Section 3. Temporary Commissioner Control

If a franchise is abandoned during the season, the Commissioner may take limited administrative action necessary to preserve competitive fairness. The purpose is to maintain a legal lineup, not to aggressively manage the abandoned team.

Section 4. Replacement Owner

A replacement owner may be appointed during the season when practical. The replacement owner inherits the franchise as it exists at the time control is transferred.

ARTICLE XI • Playoffs and Championship

Section 1. Qualification

Playoff qualification and seeding shall follow the official AFFL Sleeper settings.

Section 2. Tiebreakers

Official Sleeper tiebreakers shall control unless the AFFL establishes a different written rule before the season begins.

Section 3. League Champion

The winner of the official AFFL Championship matchup shall be recognized as the Arkansas Fantasy Football League Champion for that season.

Section 4. Championship History

The AFFL historical archive shall record, when available, the Champion, Runner-up, Championship score, Championship lineup, playoff bracket, final season record, and historical statistics.

Section 5. Permanent Recognition

A championship officially earned and recognized by the league shall remain part of AFFL history. Future rule changes shall not retroactively erase legitimate championships.

ARTICLE XII • Commissioner and Co-Commissioner

Section 1. Commissioner

The Commissioner serves as the chief administrator of the Arkansas Fantasy Football League. Responsibilities include maintaining league settings, collecting dues, organizing the draft, announcing deadlines, maintaining league records, enforcing rules, reviewing trades, resolving disputes, and presenting major issues to the league.

Section 2. Commissioner as an Owner

The Commissioner remains a full league owner and may compete, trade, draft, and manage their team like any other owner. Commissioner authority may not be used to improperly benefit the Commissioner's franchise.

Section 3. Co-Commissioner

The AFFL may maintain a Co-Commissioner to assist with league administration, rule interpretation, disputes, and situations involving a conflict of interest for the Commissioner.

Section 4. Commissioner Conflict of Interest

When an issue directly involves the Commissioner's own franchise and requires an integrity ruling, the Co-Commissioner shall provide the final decision when required by these bylaws.

ARTICLE XIII • The AFFL Council

Section 1. Purpose

The Arkansas Fantasy Football League Council exists to assist with major disputes, unusual situations, rule interpretation, league governance, and future rule development.

Section 2. Council Cases

Significant decisions may be permanently recorded in the AFFL Council Archive. Each ruling may contain a case number, season, date, issue, relevant rules, arguments, final decision, vote result, and future precedent.

Section 3. Precedent

Past Council decisions may be used as guidance in future situations. A previous ruling may be superseded by a later constitutional amendment.

ARTICLE XIV • League Voting

Section 1. Voting Rights

Each active AFFL franchise receives one vote.

Section 2. Voting Methods

Votes may be conducted using Sleeper polls, league group communication, AFFL website polling, or another clearly announced method.

Section 3. Standard Votes

Unless another requirement has already been established, normal league votes shall be decided by a simple majority of votes cast.

Section 4. Major Structural Changes

Major changes should receive substantial league support. Examples include changing league size, eliminating keepers, changing the number of keepers, changing league ownership structure, or replacing league leadership.

Section 5. Tied Votes

A tied league vote fails unless another tiebreaking procedure has been established.

Section 6. Abstentions

An abstention does not count as a vote for or against a proposal.

Section 7. Commissioner Authority

A league vote does not eliminate authority specifically granted to the Commissioner elsewhere in this Constitution. When a rule states that the Commissioner has final authority, the Commissioner retains that authority after considering league input.

ARTICLE XV • Amendments and Rule Changes

Section 1. Timing

Major rule changes should normally occur during the offseason. Rules should not be changed during the season simply because a current situation benefits or harms a particular owner.

Section 2. Emergency Changes

A rule may be changed during the season when necessary because of platform changes, an unforeseen loophole, competitive integrity concerns, or emergency league circumstances.

Section 3. Amendment History

Approved amendments should be recorded with the date, season, rule changed, and vote result or Commissioner/Council ruling when applicable. Previous versions may remain available through AFFL Headquarters for historical purposes.

ARTICLE XVI • AFFL Record Book

AFFL Headquarters shall serve as the permanent statistical archive of the Arkansas Fantasy Football League. The archive may track championships, runner-up finishes, career wins and losses, playoff records, points scored, head-to-head records, rivalries, winning and losing streaks, highest and lowest scores, biggest blowouts, closest games, draft history, transactions, annual awards, and Hall of Fame recognition.

Sleeper data shall serve as the primary source for automated league statistics.

ARTICLE XVII • Annual Awards

The AFFL may recognize awards including League Champion, Runner-Up, Regular Season King, Points King, Most Unlucky, Luckiest Schedule, Single-Game High Score, Public Execution, Photo Finish, Biggest Choke, Waiver/Free-Agent Award, Trade Award, and other awards created by the league.

Objective statistical awards may be automatically calculated using Sleeper data. Subjective awards may be determined by league vote, Commissioner decision, or Council selection.

ARTICLE XVIII • AFFL Hall of Fame

Section 1. Purpose

The AFFL Hall of Fame exists to recognize exceptional long-term achievement within the Arkansas Fantasy Football League.

Section 2. Considerations

Hall of Fame consideration may include championships, championship appearances, career wins, playoff success, scoring achievements, longevity, league records, annual awards, and historical impact.

Section 3. Résumé Score

AFFL Headquarters may display an automated Hall of Fame résumé score. This score is informational and shall not automatically induct an owner.

Section 4. Official Induction

Actual Hall of Fame induction shall follow the method established by the league. The AFFL may later establish eligibility requirements, voting threshold, annual number of inductees, waiting periods, and legacy categories.

ARTICLE XIX • Owner Conduct

Competitive trash talk, rivalries, jokes, and disagreements are normal parts of fantasy football. However, owners shall not engage in threats, serious harassment, discriminatory abuse, deliberate interference with league operations, or repeated behavior making continued participation unreasonable.

League leadership may address serious conduct issues when necessary.

ARTICLE XX • Unwritten Situations

No Constitution can anticipate every fantasy football situation. When an issue is not specifically addressed:

  1. The Commissioner shall review the facts.
  2. Existing rules shall be considered.
  3. Past AFFL Council rulings may be considered.
  4. Competitive fairness shall be prioritized.
  5. The Commissioner may consult the Co-Commissioner, Council, or league.
  6. A final ruling shall be issued.
  7. If the situation is likely to happen again, a permanent rule may be created.
The purpose of this process is not to reward technical loopholes. The goal is to reach the fairest outcome for the long-term health and integrity of the Arkansas Fantasy Football League.
ARTICLE XXI • Final Authority

This Constitution & Bylaws is the primary governing document of the Arkansas Fantasy Football League.

  1. AFFL Constitution & Bylaws
  2. Approved amendments
  3. Official AFFL Council rulings
  4. Published seasonal rules
  5. Sleeper league settings
  6. Commissioner administrative decisions

When two rules conflict, the higher authority controls unless a later rule specifically changes it.
